## 1. Legal & Licensing Framework

### Verified Open Copyright Licenses
The photographs and vector illustrations in this dataset are distributed under open licenses or in the Public Domain:
- **Public Domain (PD):** Works where copyright has naturally expired (e.g. historical scientists, classic monuments) or simple geometric/national symbols below the threshold of artistic originality.
- **Creative Commons (CC BY, CC BY-SA 2.0 / 3.0 / 4.0):** Images licensed by photographers (e.g. Gage Skidmore, Georges Biard, Bollywood Hungama) allowing free reuse with creator attribution and license links.
- **Government Open Data License (GODL-India):** Official photographic works released by the Government of India / Press Information Bureau for public reproduction.

### Unverified Legal Considerations & Practical Distinctions

#### A. Trademark Considerations (Logos & Car Brands)
All corporate, brand, and automobile logos (`lg-1` through `lg-40`) are **registered trademarks** belonging to their respective owners (e.g. BMW AG, Toyota Motor Corporation, Ford Motor Company, Apple Inc., Nike, Inc.).
- **Copyright Status:** Under US and international copyright principles (e.g. *threshold of originality*), logos consisting solely of simple geometric arrangements, standard letterforms, or basic decorative bands are not eligible for copyright protection and are treated as `Public Domain (Simple geometry / text)`.
- **Trademark Status:** They remain active trademarks. Their inclusion in this educational/arcade deduction game is solely for non-confusing, nominative identification of the brand in question. No commercial endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation is claimed or implied.

#### B. Celebrity Personality & Publicity Rights
Photographs of celebrities (`hw-1` through `hw-20`, `bw-1` through `bw-20`) are covered by Creative Commons licenses *solely as to the photographer's copyright* in the photograph. Under Indian jurisprudence (*Amitabh Bachchan v. Rajat Nagi*, Delhi HC 2022) and US right of publicity laws, celebrities retain personal rights in their name, voice, and likeness. These personality rights are **not waived** by a photographer's CC license.

#### C. Bollywood Movie Dialogues (Quotation Basis)
The 20 dialogue entries (`bd-1` through `bd-20`) feature single-sentence catchphrases.
- **Statutory Limitation:** In Indian copyright law (Section 52 of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957), "fair dealing" is an enumerated statutory defense (limited to criticism, review, private study, and news reporting) rather than an open-ended US-style fair use doctrine.
- **Legal Characterization:** These entries do **not** possess an automatic statutory license. Rather, single brief catchphrases are universally recognized under common law as *de minimis* non-copyrightable short phrases and fair quotations for quiz/trivia gameplay.
